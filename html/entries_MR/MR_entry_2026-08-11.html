I2C Pin Correction

I determined that the I2C communication failure was caused by incorrect SDA and SCL pin definitions in the firmware rather than a short circuit on the PCB. After correcting the pin numbers, the I2C scanner detected two devices on the bus, confirming that communication had been restored.

The accelerometer was not detected during this test. I suspected that the IC may have been damaged during the earlier repair attempts, so it remained a hardware issue to investigate separately.

I/O Demonstration Code

With the I2C bus operating, I began developing demonstration firmware for the Car Controller I/O hardware. The program was designed to read the push buttons and exercise the LEDs and dual 7-segment display through the I2C GPIO expander. This provided a practical way to verify the expander pin mapping and the basic operation of the user-interface components.
