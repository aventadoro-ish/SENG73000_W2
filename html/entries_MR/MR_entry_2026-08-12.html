Focus: Collaborative assembly and reflow of four Floor Controller PCBs

Floor Controller PCB Assembly

Nick, Rita, and I met to populate and reflow four additional Floor Controller PCBs. These boards contained fewer components than the previously assembled Car Controller boards, which reduced the placement workload and made the shared assembly process more efficient.

We followed the same procedure used for the earlier PCB batch: the components were organized, solder paste was applied, the parts were placed according to the assembly documentation, and the populated boards were baked using the established reflow process. The boards still required inspection, manual soldering, and corrective work before functional testing.
