Entry Snapshot

Date: 2026-08-15

Author: Matvey Regentov

Focus: Assembly and cleaning of Car Controller I/O PCBs 2 and 3

I/O PCB Assembly

I continued assembling the I/O PCBs for Car Controller units 2 and 3. On IO CC #2, I installed all currently available components except the push buttons and encoder resistors. I later noticed that I had accidentally installed 1 µF capacitors in the encoder circuit instead of the specified 0.1 µF components, so this board required a later correction.

On IO CC #3, I installed all components except the push buttons, which I did not have on hand. The missing buttons were therefore the only remaining standard components to install on that board.

PCB Cleaning

After soldering, I thoroughly cleaned both I/O PCBs. I first used isopropyl alcohol to remove flux residue and then washed the boards with soap to remove the remaining contamination. The boards were left clean and ready for the final configuration fixes and component installation.
