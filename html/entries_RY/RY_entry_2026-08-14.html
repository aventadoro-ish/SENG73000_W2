Continuing FC Board Assembly

Today, I continued assembly work on the Floor Controller (FC) boards. The focus was on completing additional component placement, including components on the back side of the PCB, while also inspecting and correcting soldering issues on the front side.

Inspecting for Solder Bridges

The FC PCB contains components on both the front and back sides. After the initial SMD population and reflow process, the next stage involved placing the remaining components and addressing visible soldering defects. In particular, I focused on identifying and fixing shorts between the pins of integrated circuits on the front side of the board.

One of the main boards I worked on was the LIF FC Rev1-03 PCB. During inspection, I identified visible solder bridges between IC pins. These shorts needed to be corrected before the board could continue through the assembly and testing process.

IC Repairs and Back-Side Population

I repaired the visible shorts on ICs U14 and U16. I also corrected the soldering on U8 to ensure the connections between the IC pins were properly formed without unintended solder bridges.

In addition to the IC soldering work, I completed the remaining soldering of eight LEDs and two 470 Ω resistors.

Accomplishments

Although I was not able to finish every remaining component placement on the board, I completed several important assembly and repair tasks. My main accomplishments for the LIF FC Rev1-03 PCB were:

  • Fixed visible solder shorts on ICs U14 and U16.
  • Corrected the soldering on U8.
  • Finished the soldering for IT.
  • Populated and soldered eight LEDs.
  • Populated and soldered two 470 Ω resistors.

Engineering Discussion

This assembly work also gave me additional experience with PCB inspection and rework. Identifying solder bridges between closely spaced IC pins required careful visual inspection and precise soldering to avoid damaging the surrounding components or PCB traces.
